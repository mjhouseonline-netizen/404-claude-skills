# Competitor Intelligence Agent

## Purpose
The Competitor Intel Agent provides deep, actionable competitive intelligence across advertising, organic search, content strategy, products, pricing, and technology. It produces a comprehensive competitive landscape report identifying threats, opportunities, and differentiation angles.

Ideal for product strategy teams, marketing leaders, and sales teams preparing for new markets or product launches.

## Capabilities
- **Ad Spend Estimation**: Estimated competitor ad budgets (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) based on creative frequency
- **Creative Analysis**: Copy analysis, messaging frameworks, visual patterns, emotional appeals, CTAs
- **SEO Comparison**: Keyword rankings, organic traffic estimation, backlink profile, content gaps
- **Content Strategy**: Blog topics, content calendar patterns, pillar content, thought leadership
- **Pricing Analysis**: Pricing tiers, bundled offerings, discounts, packaging strategy, price changes
- **Product Features**: Feature comparison matrix, unique features per competitor, feature gaps
- **Tech Stack Detection**: Website technologies, hosting, analytics, marketing tech, integrations
- **Social Media Presence**: Follower counts, engagement rates, posting frequency, content themes
- **Brand Positioning**: Value propositions, messaging hierarchy, target audiences, competitive angles
- **Market Share Indicators**: Team size, funding, hiring activity, geographic focus

## Workflow

1. **Competitor Identification Phase**
   - Define competitive landscape (direct, indirect, adjacent competitors)
   - Identify 5-10 primary competitors
   - Categorize by threat level (direct threat, emerging, niche player)
   - Validate company status (active, declining, pivoting)
   - Check acquisition status and funding

2. **Website & Tech Analysis Phase**
   - Crawl competitor websites (all major pages)
   - Identify technology stack (hosting, framework, CDN, CMS)
   - Extract meta tags and structured data
   - Identify form fields and CTAs
   - Analyze page speed and mobile experience
   - Screenshot design language and UI patterns
   - Note key messaging on homepage and pricing pages

3. **Pricing Intelligence Phase**
   - Document all pricing tiers and plans
   - Extract feature lists for each tier
   - Note billing cycles (monthly, annual, usage-based)
   - Identify any promotional pricing or discounts
   - Compare pricing strategy (freemium vs. premium, low-cost vs. premium)
   - Analyze packaging (per-user, per-site, per-transaction)
   - Note trial offer terms if available

4. **Product Feature Analysis Phase**
   - Catalog all product features mentioned
   - Build feature comparison matrix (feature-by-competitor grid)
   - Identify unique features (what only one competitor has)
   - Highlight commodity features (everyone has these)
   - Note feature roadmap mentions (jobs postings, blog posts)
   - Assess feature depth (depth of personalization, integrations, customization)

5. **Advertising Analysis Phase**
   - Run competitor domain through ad analytics tool
   - Extract all active ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok if available)
   - Analyze ad creative (messaging, visual style, CTA)
   - Identify ad variations and A/B tests running
   - Estimate monthly ad spend (based on frequency and placement data)
   - Note target audience demographics and interests
   - Identify campaign themes and seasonal patterns

6. **SEO & Content Analysis Phase**
   - Check organic keyword rankings (top 100 keywords per competitor)
   - Estimate organic traffic (tools like SEMrush, Ahrefs)
   - Identify top-performing content (most linked, most shared)
   - Analyze backlink profile (referring domain count, link authority)
   - Extract blog topics and content categories
   - Identify content gaps (topics you rank for, they don't)
   - Review content freshness (recency of updates)

7. **Social Media Analysis Phase**
   - Gather follower counts across platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, YouTube)
   - Calculate engagement rate (likes, comments, shares per post)
   - Identify posting frequency and times
   - Analyze content themes (product updates, thought leadership, customer stories)
   - Note social media content strategy (educational, promotional, entertaining)
   - Identify influencers they engage with

8. **Brand & Positioning Analysis Phase**
   - Extract key messaging (homepage headline, about page claims)
   - Identify value proposition and target audience
   - Analyze tone and voice (formal, casual, technical, creative)
   - Note brand personality and visual identity
   - Identify differentiation claims (speed, ease, affordability, innovation)
   - Review testimonials and social proof used

9. **Report Compilation Phase**
   - Create competitive matrix across all dimensions
   - Highlight competitive advantages and vulnerabilities
   - Identify market gaps and white spaces
   - Surface differentiation opportunities
   - Prioritize competitive threats by severity
   - Provide strategic recommendations

## Input Requirements
- **Target Market**: Industry and market segment
- **Direct Competitors**: 3-5 primary competitors to analyze
- **Adjacent Competitors**: 2-3 indirect/adjacent competitors
- **Analysis Scope**: Which areas to focus on (all, ads only, product only, etc.)
- **Your Product**: Brief description of your offering (context for comparison)
- **Geographic Focus**: Which geographies are relevant
- **Time Frame**: How current does analysis need to be (quarterly, annual)

## Configuration
- **Data Sources**: Configure access to SEMrush, Ahrefs, Pathmatics (ads), etc.
- **Update Frequency**: Monthly, quarterly, or annual refresh schedule
- **Competitor List**: Maintain list of primary and adjacent competitors
- **Focus Areas**: Choose which analyses to run (all, ads only, product only)

## Best Practices
1. **Monitor Continuously**: Competitive landscape shifts monthly; quarterly analysis is minimum
2. **Triangulate Data**: Use multiple sources for estimates (tools, jobs postings, social signals)
3. **Differentiate**: Use analysis to carve out unique positioning, not copy competitor
4. **Speed to Market**: Use gaps to prioritize product roadmap (fastest wins first)
5. **Pricing Power**: Understand competitor pricing to inform your pricing strategy
6. **Content Gaps**: Use competitor content analysis to identify blog/content opportunities
7. **Share Findings**: Build competitive intel into marketing, sales, and product teams

## Edge Cases
- **Private Competitors**: May not have public data on ads/traffic (use jobs postings, news for signals)
- **Bootstrapped Competitors**: May not raise funding; harder to estimate spend
- **International Competitors**: May have different pricing/features per region
- **Emerging Competitor**: New startups may have limited public presence (check jobs for direction)
