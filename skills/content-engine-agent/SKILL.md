# Content Engine Agent

## Purpose

The Content Engine Agent orchestrates the complete content creation process from topic ideation through publication and multi-channel repurposing. It handles keyword research, outline generation, draft writing, SEO optimization, and distribution across blog, landing pages, social media, and email.

Ideal for content teams scaling output, marketing teams launching campaigns, agencies managing client calendars, or founders building content moats.

---

## Capabilities

- **Topic Research**: Competitor analysis, search volume trending, content gap analysis
- **Keyword Mapping**: Primary/secondary keywords, search intent, keyword difficulty scoring
- **Outline Generation**: SERP-driven structure, user journey mapping, content hierarchy
- **Draft Writing**: AI-assisted + human refinement, brand voice consistency
- **SEO Optimization**: Meta tags, heading structure, internal linking, schema markup
- **Publishing**: CMS setup, image optimization, metadata, scheduling
- **Social Repurposing**: Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions, TikTok scripts
- **Email Integration**: Newsletter excerpts, promotional angles, nurture sequences
- **Performance Tracking**: Ranking monitoring, traffic analytics, conversion attribution

### PHASE 2: Keyword Research & Analysis (Days 2-3)

**Identify Primary & Secondary Keywords**

```
PRIMARY KEYWORD:
- Definition: Main keyword you're targeting
- Volume: [X] searches/month
- Difficulty: [0-100 scale]
- Opportunity: Volume vs. Difficulty ratio
  Good: >100 volume, <30 difficulty
  Okay: >50 volume, <50 difficulty
  Hard: <50 volume, >50 difficulty

Example: "Content marketing for SaaS"
- Volume: 1200/month
- Difficulty: 42
- Rank position to target: Position 1-3

---

SECONDARY KEYWORDS (5-10 related):
1. [Long-tail variation]: "[Primary] + [modifier]"
   Volume: [X], Difficulty: [Y]
   Example: "Content marketing for SaaS startups" (lower competition)

2. [Problem-angle variation]: "[Problem] + [solution keyword]"
   Volume: [X], Difficulty: [Y]
   Example: "How to do content marketing for SaaS" (informational)

3. [Comparison variation]: "[Primary] vs. [alternative]"
   Volume: [X], Difficulty: [Y]
   Example: "Content marketing vs. advertising for SaaS" (commercial intent)

4. [Benefit variation]: "[Primary] for [benefit]"
   Volume: [X], Difficulty: [Y]
   Example: "Content marketing for lead generation" (outcome-focused)

5. [Related concept]: "[Related] and [primary]"
   Volume: [X], Difficulty: [Y]
   Example: "SEO and content marketing strategy" (related topic)

---

KEYWORD DIFFICULTY ASSESSMENT:

If targeting keyword with difficulty 50+:
- Need domain authority >20
- Need 10+ high-quality backlinks
- Need 2500+ words of high-quality content
- Timeline to rank: 4-6 months (not weeks)

If difficulty 20-30:
- Can rank with authority >10
- 5+ backlinks helpful
- 1500+ words sufficient
- Timeline: 2-3 months

If difficulty <20:
- Easy to rank
- Few backlinks needed
- 800+ words can work
- Timeline: 2-4 weeks
```

**Long-Tail Keywords & Search Opportunities**

```
LONG-TAIL KEYWORD STRATEGY:

Single-word keywords are hard:
- "Marketing" = 50M+ results, high difficulty

Two-word combinations are easier:
- "Marketing SaaS" = 2M results, medium difficulty

Three+ word phrases are often winner:
- "Content marketing for SaaS startups" = 100K results, LOW difficulty
- "How to create content calendar" = 50K results, VERY LOW difficulty

Long-tail advantage:
- Lower competition (easier to rank)
- Higher conversion intent (people searching specific phrases know what they want)
- Lower volume but high relevance
- Multiple long-tail rankings compound to significant traffic

STRATEGY:
1. Find 1-2 medium-difficulty primary keywords (1000-5000 volume)
2. Find 5-10 long-tail secondary keywords (100-500 volume each)
3. Create content targeting primary + naturally including secondaries
4. Result: Rank for multiple keywords from single piece

Example content ranking for:
- "Content marketing for SaaS" (primary)
- "SaaS content strategy" (secondary)
- "How to create SaaS content calendar" (secondary)
- "Content marketing metrics for SaaS" (secondary)
- "Best practices for SaaS content" (secondary)
= 5 keyword rankings = 5x traffic potential
```
