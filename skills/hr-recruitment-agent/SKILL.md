# HR & Recruitment Agent

## Purpose
The HR & Recruitment Agent automates hiring process from job description through offer acceptance. It writes compelling job posts, screens candidates systematically, designs structured interviews, prepares offer packages, and optimizes hiring funnel for speed and quality.

Ideal for scaling teams quickly, improving hiring quality, and reducing time-to-hire.

## Capabilities
- **Job Description Writing**: Role, responsibilities, requirements, benefits, compensation band
- **Candidate Screening**: Auto-score resumes against requirements, flag top candidates
- **Interview Design**: Structured interviews, sample questions, evaluation rubrics
- **Offer Letter Generation**: Compensation, benefits, offer timeline, contingencies
- **Hiring Funnel Optimization**: Identify bottlenecks, measure quality of hire, predict fit
- **Diversity & Inclusion**: Reduce bias in job posts, screening, interviews
- **Background Check Coordination**: Verify credentials, check references
- **Onboarding Checklist**: First-day setup, team introductions, training

## Workflow

1. **Role Definition Phase**
   - Define role purpose (what problem does this person solve?)
   - List key responsibilities (main 5-7 areas)
   - Identify required skills (must-have vs. nice-to-have)
   - Set compensation band (market research for role/location)
   - Determine reporting structure (who do they report to?)

2. **Job Post Creation Phase**
   - Write compelling title (keywords for job search)
   - Craft opening hook (why this role is exciting)
   - Detail responsibilities (clear, specific, outcome-focused)
   - List requirements (distinguish must-have from nice-to-have)
   - Highlight benefits (compensation, flexibility, mission, growth)
   - Include call-to-action (how to apply)

3. **Distribution Phase**
   - Post on job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn, AngelList)
   - Share with network (LinkedIn, email)
   - Contact recruiters (for senior roles)
   - Source passive candidates (LinkedIn recruiter)
   - Track source (which channel brings best candidates?)

4. **Candidate Screening Phase**
   - Define scoring rubric (technical skills, culture fit, experience)
   - Auto-score resumes (quick filter of unqualified)
   - Screen top 20% (phone call or video)
   - Move qualified to interview stage
   - Communicate with candidates (status updates)

5. **Interview Design Phase**
   - Define interview structure (30-min phone, 60-min technical, 45-min culture)
   - Create question sets (same questions for all candidates, fair comparison)
   - Develop evaluation rubric (how to score each interview)
   - Prepare sample problems (practical assessment of skills)
   - Set success criteria (what does "good fit" look like?)

   - Score each candidate (numerical rating per dimension)
   - Debrief team (calibrate scores, align on decisions)
   - Reference check (verify past performance, verify story)
   - Make decision (yes/no/maybe, and how does offer rank)

7. **Offer Phase**
   - Prepare offer (role, salary, equity, benefits, start date)
   - Deliver offer (call first, then written)
   - Negotiate if needed (salary expectations, start date, etc.)
   - Get acceptance (signed offer, background clear)
   - Prepare for start (onboarding plan, equipment, team intro)

8. **Onboarding Phase**
   - First-day checklist (equipment, accounts, facilities access)
   - Week 1 plan (meetings, training, expectations)
   - 30-day check-in (how's it going? any questions?)
   - 90-day evaluation (full assessment, decision to keep)

## Input Requirements
- **Role**: Title, seniority level, team/department
- **Scope**: Replacement hire or new position? Growth?
- **Timeline**: When do you need them to start?
- **Budget**: Salary range, benefits budget
- **Team**: What's team like? Culture fit important?
- **Location**: Remote, office, hybrid? Geographic constraints?
- **Experience Level**: Entry, mid, senior? Specific background?

## Interview Process

### Phone Screen (30 min)
**Purpose**: Verify background, assess communication, gauge culture fit

**Questions**:
1. "Walk me through your most interesting project"
2. "What languages/frameworks do you use? Describe recent experience"
3. "How do you approach learning new technologies?"
4. "Why are you interested in this role?"
5. "What are your career goals?"

**Evaluation**: Does candidate communicate well? Does background match job post?

### Technical Interview (60 min)
**Purpose**: Assess coding ability, problem-solving, system design

**Interview Format**:
- 10 min: Welcome, overview of problem
- 40 min: Coding problem (live coding, shared environment)
- 10 min: Discussion of approach, trade-offs

**Sample Coding Problem**:
"Design a URL shortener system. You have 1 second to shorten a URL, 100ms latency SLA for redirects, and expect 1M URLs/day. How would you build this?"

Evaluate:
- Can they clarify requirements?
- Do they consider trade-offs (fast write vs. fast read)?
- Do they think about scale?
- Can they code a solution?

**Scoring Rubric**:
- 5: Excellent (clear thinking, good solution, considers trade-offs)
- 4: Good (correct approach, minor gaps)
- 3: Adequate (solves problem, but inefficient)
- 2: Weak (significant struggles)
- 1: Fail (can't solve)

### Culture/Team Fit Interview (45 min)
**Purpose**: Assess teamwork, communication, growth mindset

**Participants**: 2-3 team members (engineers, manager)

**Questions**:
- "Tell us about a time you disagreed with a teammate"
- "How do you handle feedback?"
- "What does good collaboration look like to you?"
- "Describe your learning style"
- "What's a time you failed? What did you learn?"

**Evaluation**: Can they work in a team? Do they grow from feedback? Are they coachable?

### Final Round (30 min)
**Purpose**: Final questions, assess overall fit

**Participants**: Hiring manager + 1 senior engineer

**Topics**:
- Compensation expectations
- Start date expectations
- Any questions for us?
- Sell the role (why they should join)

## Configuration
- **Role Level**: Entry, mid, senior (default: mid)
- **Experience Required**: Years needed (default: match to level)
- **Location**: Remote, office, hybrid (default: remote)
- **Timeline**: When to start (default: ASAP)
- **Budget**: Salary range (default: market average)

## Best Practices
1. **Structured Interviews**: Same questions for all candidates (fair comparison)
2. **Scoring Rubric**: Numerical scores (reduces bias, enables comparison)
3. **Diverse Pipeline**: Source from multiple channels (maximize diversity)
4. **Reference Checks**: Verify claims (past manager is most reliable)
5. **Speed**: Move fast (delay = candidates accept elsewhere)
6. **Transparency**: Clear about role, compensation, timeline
7. **Culture Fit**: Ensure values alignment (early indicator of success)

## Edge Cases
- **Hard-to-Fill Roles**: Specialized skills (data science, ML) require patience
- **Competitive Markets**: May need to increase salary/equity to win
- **Remote Hiring**: Harder to assess culture fit (weight team fit interview higher)
- **Diversity Goals**: Active sourcing from underrepresented groups
