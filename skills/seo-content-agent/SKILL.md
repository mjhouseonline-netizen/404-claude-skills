# SEO Content Agent

## Purpose
The SEO Content Agent produces research-backed, Google-optimized articles that rank and drive organic traffic. It handles keyword discovery through finished, published contentÃ¢â‚¬â€analyzing intent, competitive landscape, and user experience signals to create content that wins search visibility.

Ideal for content marketers, SaaS companies, agencies, and publishers building organic traffic engines.

## Capabilities
- **Keyword Research & Clustering**: Discover relevant keywords, group by intent, identify long-tail opportunities, volume/difficulty analysis
- **Search Intent Mapping**: Identify what users actually want (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional), align content format to intent
- **Competitive Analysis**: SERP analysis for top 10 results, content gap identification, length/depth benchmarks, content structure analysis
- **Content Brief Creation**: Structured outline with word count targets, key sections, data/statistics to include, expert quotes, visual recommendations
- **SEO-Optimized Article Writing**: 1500-3000 word articles with natural keyword integration, engaging narrative, clear structure, expert voice
- **Meta Title/Description Variants**: 5+ high-CTR variations per article, character limits followed, power words included
- **Internal Linking Strategy**: Contextual link recommendations to existing articles, anchor text optimization, siloing structure
- **Schema Markup Suggestions**: JSON-LD code for Article, FAQPage, Product, BreadcrumbList based on content type
- **Content Refresh Audits**: Identify outdated content, add fresh data/statistics, update internal links, republish with new publish date
- **Multimedia Planning**: Image descriptions for alt text, video transcripts, infographic concepts, visual assets required
- **CTA Integration**: Call-to-action placement (top, middle, bottom), conversion path mapping, lead magnet recommendations

## Workflow

1. **Keyword Research & Opportunity Identification**
   - Identify primary keyword (target phrase, 20-100 monthly searches)
   - Generate 50+ related keywords using keyword research tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Ubersuggest)
   - Analyze keyword difficulty (KD < 50 for easier wins)
   - Identify long-tail variations (3-5 word phrases, 10-50 monthly searches, low competition)
   - Analyze search volume trends (growing vs. declining keywords)
   - Identify seasonal opportunities
   - Group keywords into clusters (primary + related terms)
   - Flag commercial intent keywords (high purchase probability)

2. **Search Intent & Content Format Assessment**
   - Determine primary search intent: Informational (learn), Commercial (compare), Navigational (find service), Transactional (buy)
   - Analyze top 10 SERP results for format patterns:
     - Blog post vs. guide vs. comparison vs. tool vs. video
     - Average word count (aim for +10% above median)
     - Visual assets used (infographics, data visualizations, videos)
     - Heading hierarchy and section structure
   - Identify featured snippet format (list, table, definition, step-by-step)
   - Check for SERP features: knowledge panel, local pack, ads, People Also Ask, related searches
   - Determine optimal content format for intent + competitive landscape

3. **Competitive Deep-Dive Analysis**
   - Analyze top 3 ranking articles in detail:
     - Content length, depth, comprehensiveness
     - Key sections and topics covered
     - Data sources and citations
     - Internal link strategy
     - Visual assets and multimedia
     - Readability and structure
   - Identify content gaps: topics covered by competitors vs. what's missing
   - Find quick wins: sections that can be improved with fresher data, better explanations, or more depth
   - Analyze backlink profiles of top articles (where their links come from)
   - Review People Also Ask questions (content expansion opportunities)
   - Check related searches at bottom of SERP (adjacent topics to cover)

4. **Content Brief Creation**
   - Define primary keyword and related keywords to target
   - Set word count target (typically 500-1500 for product/guide, 2000-3000 for pillar content)
   - Create detailed outline with H2/H3 sections
   - Assign section word counts (more words in high-value sections)
   - List required statistics, data points, expert quotes (with sources)
   - Identify visual assets needed (infographics, screenshots, stock photos, videos)
   - Define featured snippet optimization target (if applicable)
   - Specify CTA placement and link targets
   - Note schema markup requirements

5. **Article Drafting & SEO Optimization**
   - Write engaging introduction (hook, keyword in first 100 words, value proposition)
   - Structure with clear H2 subheadings (1 per 300 words, keyword-relevant)
   - Use H3 subheadings for complex sections
   - Integrate primary keyword naturally: title (1), intro (1), H2 (2-3), body (3-5), conclusion (1) = 8-11 natural uses
   - Create transition sentences that include secondary keywords
   - Use bold/italics for emphasis on key concepts
   - Build tables, lists, comparison matrices for scanability
   - Include data, statistics, expert quotes with attribution
   - Write compelling conclusion with benefit summary
   - Add clear CTA aligned to conversion goal (email signup, free trial, consultation request)
   - Optimize for readability: short sentences (15-20 words avg), short paragraphs (2-3 sentences), subheadings every 300 words

6. **Meta Optimization & Markup**
   - Create 5+ meta title variations (55-60 characters including keyword)
   - Create 5+ meta description variations (150-160 characters, benefit + CTA)
   - Test power words (Best, Ultimate, Complete, Beginner, Advanced)
   - Add schema markup (JSON-LD) for Article type
   - Add FAQPage schema if content answers user questions
   - Optimize image alt text for primary keyword and relevant variations
   - Create internal link anchor text with keyword variation

7. **Internal Linking & Site Integration**
   - Identify 5-10 internal link opportunities to relevant articles
   - Use contextual anchor text with keyword relevance
   - Link to authority content (older articles with established ranking)
   - Ensure no orphaned content (every article linked from elsewhere)
   - Create siloing structure if multi-category site (category > subtopic > article)
   - Update existing articles to link to new content (backlink authority transfer)

8. **Content Refresh & Publication**
   - Audit existing similar content for refresh vs. new publish decision
   - If refreshing: add fresher data, update examples, improve structure, add new sections
   - Update publish date to signal freshness to Google
   - Rewrite meta description if unchanged
   - Add new internal links from updated content

## Input Requirements
- **Target Keyword or Topic**: Primary keyword or topic area to create content about
- **Domain/Site Context**: Industry, target audience, existing content library
- **Competitor URLs** (optional): Up to 3 competitor URLs ranking for the keyword
- **Content Goals**: Traffic target, conversion goal, business objective
- **Depth Preference**: Quick article (500 words), standard (1500 words), comprehensive pillar (3000+ words)
- **Content Type**: Blog post, guide, comparison, tutorial, tool review, case study
- **Target Audience**: Specific persona, experience level, pain point

## Configuration
- **Word Count Range**: Adjust based on industry (tech: 2000-3000, e-commerce: 1500-2000, SaaS: 1500-2500)
- **Keyword Integration**: Conservative (natural) vs. aggressive (densely optimized)
- **Internal Link Ratio**: 1 link per 300-500 words is typical
- **Image Frequency**: 1 image per 500-800 words maintains engagement
- **CTA Placement**: Top (awareness stage), middle (consideration), bottom (decision)

## Best Practices
1. **User-First Approach**: Write for humans first, SEO second. Google rewards content that satisfies user intent.
2. **Keyword Clustering**: Don't create multiple articles targeting the same keyword intent; cluster into one comprehensive article.
3. **Freshness Updates**: Refresh top-performing articles every 12 months with new data and publish date update.
4. **E-E-A-T Signals**: Demonstrate Expertise (byline), Experience (case studies), Authoritativeness (backlinks), Trustworthiness (citations).
5. **Mobile First**: Structure content for mobile scanning (short paragraphs, clear headings, visual breaks).
6. **Topic Clusters**: Create pillar articles (3000+ words) linked to 5-10 cluster articles (1500 words) on related subtopics.
7. **Link Building Plan**: For each article, plan 3-5 backlink targets (blogs to pitch, directories, resource lists).
8. **Competitive Content+**: Match competitor depth but add original research, data, or unique perspective.
9. **CTA Conversion Path**: Lead articles Ã¢â€ â€™ email signup or free resource. Commercial articles Ã¢â€ â€™ free trial or demo request.
10. **Analytics Loop**: Track top search keywords, content gaps, and user behavior to inform next article selection.

## Edge Cases
- **Broad Topics**: Break into multiple articles (pillar + clusters) rather than 1 comprehensive article
- **New Topics**: If no search volume, create educational content to establish authority before commercial demand appears
- **Highly Competitive Keywords**: Target long-tail variants (KD < 50) first, then upgrade to main keyword after establishing authority
- **E-A-T Sensitive**: Health, finance, legal topics require expert bylines, citations, and trustworthy sources
- **Evergreen vs. News**: Evergreen content (timeless) vs. news pegs (trending moments); plan mix of both
- **Multilingual Sites**: Hreflang implementation required; create separate content per language, not translated copies

## Integration Points
- **Content Calendar**: Insert into editorial calendar with publication date and promotion plan
- **Email Nurture**: Extract key sections for email sequences (email series based on article chapters)
- **Social Repurposing**: Break article into 10+ social posts (key stats, tips, quotes)
- **Webinar Topics**: Long-form articles become webinar outlines
- **Podcast Scripts**: Article structure becomes podcast episode outline
- **Video Scripts**: Article sections become YouTube video scripts
- **Link Building**: Article becomes pitch-worthy asset for relationship building and backlinks
