## Workflow

1. **Social Presence Audit & Strategy Phase**
   - Audit current social profiles (followers, engagement rate, posting frequency, audience demographics)
   - Identify brand voice and content pillars (max 5 pillars)
   - Research target audience by platform (where they are, what they engage with)
   - Benchmark against 3-5 competitors on each platform
   - Define goals: audience growth target, engagement rate baseline, lead generation targets
   - Choose platform mix (which platforms to prioritize, which are secondary)

2. **Content Calendar & Strategy Building**
   - Create 30-day rolling content calendar with themes, topics, and platform assignments
   - Define posting frequency per platform (LinkedIn: 3-5x/week, Twitter: 2-5x/day, Instagram: 1-3x/week, TikTok: 3-5x/week)
   - Map content to business objectives (awareness, consideration, conversion, retention)
   - Plan seasonal campaigns and trending moments (Q2 launch, back-to-school, holiday)
   - Build hashtag banks for each platform (20-50 relevant tags per platform)
   - Create content templates per platform type (carousel posts, educational threads, behind-the-scenes, case studies)

3. **Content Drafting & Formatting Phase**
   - Draft content batches (weekly or bi-weekly) with platform-specific formatting
   - LinkedIn: Long-form articles (1000-1500 words), carousels (5-9 slides), text posts (150-300 words)
   - Twitter/X: Threads (7-15 tweets), standalone hot takes, conversation starters
   - Instagram: Captions (150-300 words), carousel copy, Stories scripts, Reels hooks
   - TikTok: Video scripts (30-60 seconds), hook-body-CTA structure, trending audio integration
   - Facebook: Community-focused posts (100-200 words), event promotions, carousel ads
   - Create 5+ headline/hook variants per major post for A/B testing
   - Add CTAs (Learn more, Sign up, Join waitlist, Comment with X, Share this)
   - Tag relevant accounts and use platform-specific mentions

4. **Engagement & Community Strategy Phase**
   - Set engagement targets (reply rate, mention rate, share frequency)
   - Create comment response templates (Q&A, acknowledgment, redirect to DM)
   - Define escalation protocol (when to use DMs, when to tag team members)
   - Plan community engagement calendar (reply windows, live Q&As, engagement sprints)
   - Build influencer/collaborator outreach list (50+ people per platform)
   - Design engagement incentive mechanics (contests, challenges, AMAs)

5. **Performance Monitoring & Optimization Phase**
   - Track key metrics: reach, impressions, engagement rate, audience growth, click-through rate
   - Analyze top performers (content type, time, headline, visuals that win)
   - Identify underperformers and adjust strategy (topic, format, time, frequency)
   - Test optimal posting times per platform (analyze audience timezone distribution)
   - Audit audience sentiment (positive/negative comments, pain points mentioned)
   - Monthly reporting: growth, top posts, audience demographics, revenue attributed

6. **Cross-Platform Repurposing & Scaling Phase**
   - Convert successful LinkedIn articles into Twitter threads
   - Turn blog posts into Instagram carousel posts
   - Adapt written content into TikTok/Reels scripts
   - Create quote graphics from top content pieces
   - Develop content series that span platforms (Week 1 LinkedIn deep-dive, Week 2 Twitter thread, Week 3 Instagram carousel)
   - Build audience segments by platform (fans who engage on LinkedIn vs. TikTok) and tailor messaging

## Input Requirements
- **Brand/Business Context**: Industry, target customer, key differentiators
- **Content Pillars**: 3-5 core topics/themes (e.g., product education, company culture, thought leadership)
- **Target Audience**: Demographics, pain points, interests, location
- **Current Social Presence**: Existing accounts (if any), follower counts, recent content examples
- **Goals**: Audience growth target (followers/month), engagement targets, lead generation goals, revenue targets
- **Platform Priority**: Which platforms to focus on (primary vs. secondary)
- **Content Preferences**: Tone, formats (video, images, text), educational vs. entertaining balance
- **Timeline**: When to launch, content velocity (posts/week), campaign dates

## Configuration
- **Posting Schedule**: Customize per platform based on audience timezone analysis
- **Content Mix**: Adjust template balance (e.g., 50% educational, 30% entertaining, 20% promotional)
- **Hashtag Strategy**: Research relevant tags weekly; remove underperforming ones monthly
- **Community Engagement**: Set response time SLAs (same-day for comments, 2-hour for DMs)
- **Performance Tracking**: Connect analytics tools (Hootsuite, Later, native platform analytics)
- **Platform Priorities**: Allocate effort (e.g., 40% LinkedIn, 30% Twitter, 30% Instagram)

## Best Practices
1. **Consistency Over Perfection**: Regular posting beats perfect-but-infrequent content
2. **Platform Native First**: Never auto-post identical content; adapt per platform norms
3. **Engagement Before Reach**: Build 1,000 engaged followers instead of 10,000 passive ones
4. **Repurposing System**: One pillar content piece = 10+ social posts across platforms
5. **Community First**: Reply to 5x more posts than you create; listen to your audience
6. **Data-Driven Optimization**: Weekly analysis of top posts; replicate successful formats
7. **Seasonal Planning**: Plan campaigns 4-6 weeks in advance
8. **Story Consistency**: Same voice/message across platforms; different formats
9. **Creator Partnerships**: 1 collaboration per month drives exponential reach
10. **Trending Integration**: Use 2-3 trending audio/topics per week (TikTok/Reels)

## Edge Cases
- **Niche Audiences**: If targeting very specific audience, focus on LinkedIn + niche communities before TikTok
- **B2B vs B2C**: B2B = LinkedIn focus, B2C = Instagram/TikTok focus, both = Twitter community building
- **Seasonal Businesses**: Adjust content calendar for off-season (educational content, team stories)
- **Personal Brand**: Prioritize authenticity and consistency over production quality
- **Crisis Response**: Prepare holding statements and escalation protocol before issues arise
- **Content Gaps**: Fill gaps with community questions, trending topics, competitor analysis
- **Algorithm Changes**: Diversify content types to hedge against platform algorithm shifts
